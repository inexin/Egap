The document's optional content groups (8.11), its layers, from the catalog's /OCProperties /OCGs, each once in the order listed. Removing a layer takes it out of the configurations; the content it marks stays on the pages unless Remove(PdfLayer, bool) is asked to remove it too.

Remove(PdfLayer)

Removes a layer from /OCGs and from every configuration, the default and the alternates in /Configs: their /ON, /OFF, /Order, /Locked, /RBGroups and /AS lists. A viewer then no longer lists it; content marked with it is shown or hidden as content of no layer would be.
