A page's annotations (12.5): its /Annots array, in order, each annotation once. Entries that are not dictionaries are left out, as pdf.js and PDFium leave them out. Adding and removing edit the array in place, keep each annotation's /P pointing at the page, and keep popups with the annotations they belong to.

Flatten(PdfFlattenFilter, bool)

Flattens the page's annotations (Phase 2 Q13): their appearances are drawn into the page's content and the annotations removed, with their popups. Widgets are left to Flatten(IEnumerable<PdfField>?), links are left unless asked for, and hidden annotations are left too. A missing appearance is drawn first where Egap draws the subtype (UpdateAppearance()); an annotation still without one is removed without being drawn.

public int Flatten(PdfFlattenFilter filter = PdfFlattenFilter.All, bool includeLinks = false)
