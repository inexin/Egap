What Optimize(PdfOptimizeOptions?, CancellationToken) does (Phase 7 design Q2 to Q7). A preset fills in the switches; each can be changed with with. Lossless, the default, never changes a pixel: it merges duplicates, subsets fonts, removes what nobody sees, codes bilevel images as CCITT G4, optimizes JPEG Huffman tables and writes object streams. Print, Ebook and Screen also downsample images above 1.5 times their resolution and write photos as JPEG at their quality.
