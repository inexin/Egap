Class DocumentBuilder

Egap.Documents

Writes into a document at a cursor (Phase 6 design Q3, as Aspose.Words' DocumentBuilder): text, paragraphs, breaks, tables, pictures, fields, hyperlinks and bookmarks. The cursor sits in a paragraph, before an inline or at its end; Font and ParagraphFormat give what is written its direct formatting.

Starts a group of blocks before the cursor's paragraph and moves the cursor into it: what is written until EndGroup() goes inside, and the group's Paging says how it is placed across pages (kept together, given room, shown entire). Groups nest.

Examples

builder.Writeln("Subtotal: 1 200");
builder.Write("Total: 1 500");
builder.EndGroup();
