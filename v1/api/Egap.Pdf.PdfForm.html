The interactive form (12.7): a thin view over the catalog's /AcroForm dictionary, whose /Fields are the roots of the field tree. A document without one has no fields. Fields are read leniently: loops, entries that are not dictionaries and trees deeper than 64 levels are skipped, as pdf.js and qpdf skip them.

Flattens fields (Phase 2 Q13): their widgets' appearances are drawn into the pages' content and the fields and widgets removed; with no fields given, all of them, and the form itself. Missing appearances are drawn first, and all text and choice appearances are redrawn when NeedAppearances asks viewers to; hidden widgets are removed without being drawn.
