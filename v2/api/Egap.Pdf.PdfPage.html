A page (7.7.3.3): a thin view over the page's dictionary, which it reads and writes directly, so anything it does not cover is still there in Dictionary. Two views are equal when they wrap the same dictionary (Phase 2 Q1).

Remarks

Resources, MediaBox, CropBox and Rotate are inherited from the page tree when the page has none (7.7.3.4). Setting one writes it on the page itself. The boxes read are the ones that take effect (14.11.2): the crop box is clipped to the media box, and the bleed, trim and art boxes default to the crop box and are clipped to the media box. A missing or unusable media box is US Letter, as pdf.js and PDFium take it.

AddResource(PdfName, PdfObject)

Adds a resource, such as a font, image or graphics state, to the page and returns the name content uses for it (Phase 3 design Q6). Resources that are inherited or that other pages may share are first copied onto the page, shallowly, so other pages never change. A reference the category already holds keeps its name; a new resource is named Eg, a letter for its category (EgX for XObjects, EgG for graphics states, EgF for fonts) and a counter, clear of every name already there.

Flush()

Moves the page's finished content out of memory (Phase 3 design, after Phase 2 Q14): its content streams, and the images, forms, patterns and shadings its resources and annotation appearances use, go to the document's scratch file, and a save copies them from there. For generating many pages: draw a page, dispose its canvas, flush it, and memory holds only small dictionaries. Font subsets stay open until the save, as later pages may add glyphs. The page can still be read and changed afterwards; its streams are then read back from the scratch file.

SetContent(PdfContent)

Makes content the page's content (Phase 3 design Q4). Content read from this page with GetContent() keeps every stream whose operations are unchanged, byte for byte, and writes only new and changed operations, compactly, into new streams; unchanged content changes nothing. Other content replaces the page's in one new stream. New streams are compressed unless the page's streams were all uncompressed.

ToFormXObject(PdfDocument)

Makes the page a form XObject in target (Phase 7 design Q8), for drawing it inside other pages: its content and resources, with its crop box as the bounding box and its rotation and user unit folded into the form's matrix, so the form shows the page as a viewer does. The appearances of annotations that print (stamps, filled fields, drawings) are drawn into it; links, fields without appearances, annotations that do not print and the page's tags are left out, each with a warning. Resources are copied once per source document and shared by every form made from it.

public PdfFormXObject ToFormXObject(PdfDocument target)
