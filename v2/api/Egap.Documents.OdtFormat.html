Class OdtFormat

Namespace
Egap.Documents
Assembly
Egap.Documents.dll

OpenDocument Text (ODF 1.2 to 1.4) read into the model: ODT documents, OTT templates and flat FODT files, with their styles, lists, tables, frames, sections, page styles, headers and footers, notes, comments, fields, bookmarks, links and tracked changes. Charts, formulas and other embedded objects are kept as islands drawn as their stored picture.
