Who signs and with which key (Phase 5 design Q3): a certificate with its private key, or any RSA or ECDsa object with the certificate it belongs to. A subclass of RSA or ECDsa reaches a smart card, an HSM or a cloud key service, since Egap only ever asks it to sign the signed attributes. A signature made somewhere else entirely goes through PrepareSignatureAsync(string, PdfSignOptions, Stream, CancellationToken) instead.
