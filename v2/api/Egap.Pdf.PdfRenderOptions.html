How a page is rendered (Phase 4 design Q3 and Q4): the resolution or a size to fit, which box shows, extra rotation, a region of the output for tiles, the intent, the background, annotations and anti-aliasing. The defaults draw the crop box at 72 dpi (one pixel per point) on white with annotations, as a viewer shows the page.
