A form field (12.7.4): a thin view over its field dictionary. Terminal fields are typed by /FT and their flags: PdfTextField, PdfCheckBoxField, PdfRadioButtonField, PdfPushButtonField, PdfComboBoxField, PdfListBoxField and PdfSignatureField; non-terminal fields, and terminal fields of an unknown type, are plain PdfFields. Two views are equal when they wrap the same dictionary.

Remarks

Inheritable entries (/FT, /Ff, /V, /DV, /DA, /Q and /Opt) are read from the field or the nearest ancestor that has them through /Parent, as qpdf and pdf.js read them; /DA and /Q fall back to the form's. A kid without a name (/T) that is a widget is one of its parent's widgets, not a field of its own.
