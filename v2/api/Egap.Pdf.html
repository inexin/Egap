PdfContent

PdfContentWalker

Derive from it and override the On methods to see paths, glyphs, images, shadings and marked content with the state they are drawn in (Phase 3 design Q3).

PdfContentWriter

Writes a content stream (8.2) operator by operator, every operator of ISO 32000-2 table A.1 included, into bytes for a page, form XObject or appearance stream. Operands are written as the object writer writes objects, so names and strings are escaped, and numbers have no exponents: whole numbers as integers, others rounded to five decimals, or to six significant digits below 1. Saves, text objects and marked content are counted, and ToArray() refuses content that leaves one open, which a viewer would misread.

PdfOptimizeOptions

Print, Ebook and Screen also downsample images above 1.5 times their resolution and write photos as JPEG at their quality.

PdfXfa

The XFA form of a document (12.7.8, hub Q7): the form's /XFA, one XDP stream or an array of named packets. Egap keeps it on save as it is, reads its packets, and keeps the datasets packet in step with the AcroForm field values: setting a field's value writes it into the data too. A static XFA form converts to a plain AcroForm with ConvertToAcroForm(); a dynamic one, which has no AcroForm fields of its own to fall back on, is reported by IsDynamic and not converted.

Structs

Length

A length on the page, held in points (1/72 inch) and made from points, millimetres, centimetres or inches, so sizes in different units cannot be mixed up. It converts to and from a double in points without a cast, so it can be passed wherever a size in points is asked for, and a plain number still works wherever a Length is.
