Class Chart

Namespace
Egap.Documents.Charts
Assembly
Egap.Documents.dll

A chart, as Excel, Word and PowerPoint keep one in its own part: a DrawingML chart (c:) or one of the newer types (cx:). You can read, change and write what the properties name; everything else in the part stays as the file had it, and a chart you did not change is saved byte for byte. Charts come from a workbook's drawings, from a Word document's islands, or from Create(ChartType, bool).
