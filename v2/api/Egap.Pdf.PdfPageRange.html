A choice of pages written as text, such as "1,3,5-10,z", that is turned into page numbers once the page count is known. Pass it to ExtractPages(PdfPageRange), PageRange and the page renderers, or turn it into page indices with Resolve(int) for Import(PdfDocument, IEnumerable<int>?, int?, PdfImportOptions?).

Examples

using PdfDocument first = source.ExtractPages(PdfPageRange.Parse("1-3,z"));
IReadOnlyList<int> indices = PdfPageRange.Parse("2-z:even").Resolve(source.Pages.Count);

Remarks

The text is a comma-separated list of parts. A part is a page number (1 is the first page), z for the last page, r and a number counting from the end (r1 is the last page, r2 the one before), or a range of two of these joined by a dash. A range runs backwards when its first page comes after its last ("10-1"). A part can end in :odd or :even to keep every other page of it, counted within the part. A part that starts with x takes its pages out of what the earlier parts chose ("1-z,x3-4"). An empty text or "all" chooses every page.

Pages are kept in the order written and may repeat, so "1,1" chooses the first page twice.
