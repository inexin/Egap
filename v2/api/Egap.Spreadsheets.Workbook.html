Methods

Calculate()

Calculates the formulas that need it, as Excel's F9 does: those reading a cell whose value or formula changed since the last calculation, the volatile ones (NOW, RAND, INDIRECT and the like), and the formulas reading their results, each after what it reads. A loaded workbook keeps the results Excel saved until its cells change; one changed since it was loaded or made has every formula calculated the first time. Independent formulas are calculated in parallel when Concurrent is on, and circular references iterate when Iterate is. A function Egap does not have gives #NAME?.

ToDocument()

The workbook as a document: a section per visible sheet, with the sheet's name as a heading and a table of its values as displayed. Formulas show the value they hold now; hidden rows and columns, and rows and columns that show nothing, are left out, and the layout finds column widths from the text. Merged cells stay merged; bold, italic, alignment and links carry over. A sheet is cut off at two million table cells, with a warning.

public Document ToDocument()
