Table of Contents

Namespace Egap.Presentations

Classes

AutoShape

A shape with a geometry, a fill and an outline, and usually text (p:sp): rectangles, text boxes, placeholders.

ColorMap

A colour map: which theme colour each of the twelve colour names of a slide stands for. A master has one; a layout or slide may override it, as a dark title slide swaps the background and text colours.

ConnectorShape

A connector (p:cxnSp): a line or elbow between two shapes, which follows them when they move.

GraphicFrame

A frame for a table, chart, SmartArt diagram or embedded object (p:graphicFrame). What it holds is kept as it is and written back; Kind says what it is.

GroupShape

A group of shapes (p:grpSp), moved and sized as one. Its shapes are placed in its child coordinates (ChildArea), which map onto the group's own Transform.

PictureShape

A picture (p:pic): an image from a part of the file, cropped and framed.

Presentation

A PowerPoint presentation: its slides, the slide layouts they follow and the slide masters those belong to. A loaded presentation keeps its file whole, so a save writes back every part and every element the model does not hold, as it was, and only the parts whose content the model changed are written again.

PresentationLoadOptions

How a presentation is loaded.

PresentationTheme

A slide master's theme (a:theme): its twelve colours and its heading and body fonts, as read from the file. It is read-only here; the theme part is written back as it was.

ShapePlaceholder

The placeholder a shape fills: its type and index, which match it to its layout's and master's placeholders.

Slide

A slide: the layout it follows, its shapes, and whether the show skips it.

SlideBase

What slides, slide layouts and slide masters have in common: a part of the file, a name, and a tree of shapes (p:cSld).

SlideCollection

The slides of a presentation, in the order they are shown. A slide the presentation lists whose part is missing from the file is left out; its entry is written back as it was.

SlideLayout

A slide layout: the placeholders and shapes slides that follow it start from, under one master.

SlideMaster

A slide master: the theme, colour map, text styles and shapes its layouts and their slides start from.

SlideShape

A shape on a slide, layout or master: an AutoShape, PictureShape, GroupShape, ConnectorShape or GraphicFrame. Its id, name and position are read from the file and written back where they change; everything else the shape holds is kept as it was.

SlideShapeCollection

The shapes of a slide, layout, master or group, in the order they are drawn: the first is at the back. A shape written two ways in mc:AlternateContent, for newer and older readers, is read from the branch this reader understands; a change is made in that branch only, and the other keeps the values it had.

TextBody

A shape's text (p:txBody): its paragraphs and their runs. The body's and paragraphs' properties, bullets and styles are kept as they are.

TextParagraph

A paragraph of a shape's text (a:p): its runs and line breaks, and its outline level.

TextRun

A run of text with one formatting (a:r), or a field PowerPoint fills in, such as the slide number (a:fld).

Structs

ShapeConnection

Where a connector end is attached: a shape's id and the connection site on it, numbered from 0.

ShapeTransform

A shape's position, size, rotation and flips, in EMUs (English Metric Units: 914,400 to the inch, 12,700 to the point), with the position and size in points alongside.

SlideSize

The size of a slide or notes page, in EMUs (English Metric Units: 914,400 to the inch, 12,700 to the point).

Enums

ColorMapSlot

The colour names slides use for text and backgrounds, which a ColorMap maps to theme colours (ECMA-376 Part 1, 19.3.1.6, p:clrMap).

GraphicFrameKind

What a GraphicFrame holds.

PlaceholderType

The kinds of placeholder (ECMA-376 Part 1, 19.7.10, ST_PlaceholderType).

PresentationFormat

The kinds of PowerPoint file: presentations, templates and shows, with or without macros.

SlideLayoutType

The kinds of slide layout PowerPoint uses to pick a layout for new content (ECMA-376 Part 1, 19.7.15, ST_SlideLayoutType).
