Namespace Egap.Documents.Comparing

Classes

DocumentChange

One difference between the two documents, in the result document's order: what changed, the text before and after, and the tracked changes in Document that hold it, to accept or reject on their own.

DocumentCompare

Compares two documents and returns a third that holds every difference as a tracked change, like Word's Compare. The documents may come from any format Egap reads, also from two different formats, as every reader fills the same model.

DocumentCompareOptions

Settings for Run(Document, Document, DocumentCompareOptions?); the defaults match Word's Compare.

DocumentCompareStatistics

Counts of what a comparison found.

DocumentCompareWarning

Something a comparison could not show exactly, with how many times it happened.

DocumentComparison

The result of Run(Document, Document, DocumentCompareOptions?): a new document holding every difference as a tracked change, and the list of those differences.
