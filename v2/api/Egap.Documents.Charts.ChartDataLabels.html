Position

Where labels sit against their points, or null for the chart's default. Excel refuses a file with a position its chart type does not allow: clustered columns and bars take center, inside end, inside base and outside end; stacked ones the same without outside end; line, scatter, bubble and stock charts take top, bottom, left, right and center; pies take best fit, center, inside end and outside end; and radar, area, doughnut, surface and other 3D charts take none.
