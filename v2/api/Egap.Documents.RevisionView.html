How tracked changes show on pages, in images and PDFs, and in the HTML, EPUB and Markdown written.

public enum RevisionView

Fields

Final = 0

As if every change were accepted: inserted text shows, deleted text does not. The default.

Markup = 1

Every change shows, in its author's colour: inserted text underlined, deleted text struck through and still taking its space, text whose formatting changed in its new look with a dotted underline, and a bar in the outside margin beside each line with a change.

Original = 2

As if every change were rejected: the document before the changes.
