ConvertColor

Gets whether colour is converted to sRGB. With false, gray, RGB and CMYK images (ICCBased ones by their component count, Indexed ones by their base) keep their components as stored: a CMYK image gives CMYK pixels, without its mask, as Egami has no CMYK pixel with alpha. Other spaces are converted either way. Defaults to true.

public bool ConvertColor { get; init; }
