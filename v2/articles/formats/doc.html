DOC

Egap.Documents reads Word's binary documents and templates, the DOC and DOT files of Word 97 to 2003, and those of Word 6 and Word 95. It needs no add-on package. Egap does not write DOC: save a document read from one as DOCX, or in any other format Egap writes.

Reading

Document.Load reads a DOC file, and Document.Identify reports it as DocumentFormatKind.Doc. Both go by the file's bytes: a compound file ([MS-CFB]) with a WordDocument stream is a binary Word file, whatever its extension.

using Egap.Documents;

Document letter = Document.Load("letter.doc", out IReadOnlyList<ConversionWarning> warnings);
DocumentFormatKind kind = Document.Identify("letter.doc");        // Doc, or Encrypted for a password-protected one
letter.SaveAsDocx("letter.docx");

The reader follows [MS-DOC] and what Word writes. It reads:

  • The text through the piece table, fast-saved files included, in Unicode or in the file's code page.
  • Character and paragraph formatting, the style sheet, fonts, tab stops, borders and shading.
  • Lists from the list and list override tables, and the paragraph numbering of Word 6 and Word 95.
  • Tables, nested tables and merged cells.
  • Sections with their page setup and columns, and their headers and footers.
  • Footnotes and endnotes, comments with their ranges, authors and initials, fields, bookmarks, and tracked insertions and deletions with their authors and dates.
  • The document settings Word keeps in the file (the DOP), such as the default tab stop, track changes and automatic hyphenation with its zone, line limit and capitals switch.
  • Pictures in the line (PNG, JPEG, EMF, WMF, Macintosh PICT, device-independent bitmaps and TIFF) with their size, scale and cropping.
  • Floating pictures, text boxes, rectangles, ellipses and other simple shapes from the file's drawings ([MS-ODRAW]), placed as Word places them, and the members of shape groups.

What the reader cannot place is left out with a ConversionWarning: drawing shapes Egap has no kind for (WordArt, for one), pictures in a format it does not read, and the headers, footers and drawings of Word 6 and Word 95 files.

Passwords

A DOC file protected with a password to open it is Encrypted to Document.Identify, and loads with its password. DocumentLoadOptions takes the password, or a callback that asks for one; a missing or wrong password throws a DocumentPasswordException. The reader opens the three kinds of protection Word 97 and later write: RC4 CryptoAPI encryption (the default from Word 2002 on), RC4 encryption ("Office 97/2000 compatible") and XOR obfuscation ("weak encryption").

using Egap.Documents;

Document report = Document.Load("report.doc", new DocumentLoadOptions { Password = "secret" });
Document asked = Document.Load("report.doc", new DocumentLoadOptions
{
    PasswordCallback = attempt => attempt <= 3 ? Console.ReadLine() : null,    // null gives up
});

Password-protected Word 6 and Word 95 files are refused with a DocumentException.

From the command line

egap convert reads DOC files like any other input: to PDF, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, Markdown or text. --password opens a password-protected one.

egap convert letter.doc -o letter.pdf
egap convert old/*.doc -o converted/ --to docx
egap convert report.doc -o report.docx --password-env REPORT_PASSWORD

Known limits

  • Word 2 and earlier, and Word 4 and 5 for the Macintosh, are not read.
  • Formatting revisions (a tracked change of formatting) are not read; insertions and deletions are.
  • OLE objects come in as the picture the file keeps for them; their data is not read.
  • Theme colours and fonts are what Word saved for them: a binary file keeps the colour, not the theme slot.
  • Headers, footers and drawings of Word 6 and Word 95 files are left out with a warning, and so are their comment ranges.

Limits

DocumentLoadOptions.Limits keeps an untrusted file in bounds: MaxPartSize caps each stream of the compound file and the text, MaxEntries caps tables such as the paragraph count of a part and the list and style tables, and MaxDepth caps how deep tables nest. A file past a limit throws a DocumentLimitException; a damaged file throws a DocumentException.

using Egap.Documents;

DocumentLoadOptions options = new() { Limits = new DocumentLimits { MaxPartSize = 16 * 1024 * 1024 } };
Document upload = Document.Load("upload.doc", options);
