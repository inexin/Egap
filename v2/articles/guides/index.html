Guides

Each guide covers one area of Egap: what it does, the options and their defaults, and the limits. Some guides end with Recipes, short complete answers to common tasks such as merging files or adding page numbers, with code you can copy.

The guides are grouped by package, and within a group they are in alphabetical order. Each document format has its own page in Document formats. If you are new, read Getting started first, and Packages to pick what to install.

Egap.Pdf

Reading, editing, rendering and saving PDF files.

  • Editing PDF files: pages, metadata, outlines, annotations and forms; damaged and encrypted files; saving.
  • Fonts: the open fonts that stand in for missing ones, in Egap.Pdf and Egap.Documents alike.
  • PDF tools: optimize, stamps and imposition, page ranges, compare and clean-up.
  • Print production: preflight against a print profile, separations and ink coverage, colour conversion, PDF/X and overprint preview.
  • Redaction and PDF/A: true redaction, replacing text, PDF/A and e-invoices.
  • Rendering pages: pages to images, or onto any Egami canvas.
  • Signatures: signing, verifying, and keys on smart cards and HSMs.
  • Text, images and drawing: text extraction, finding text, images, and drawing on a page.

Egap.Documents

One document model for DOCX, HTML, Markdown and plain text, its page layout, and conversions to and from PDF.

  • Flow documents: the document model, editing, text options, barcodes, mail merge and page layout.
  • Moving to Egap.Documents 2.0: the new packages and namespaces, and what else changed.
  • PDF export and conversion: documents to PDF, one-line conversions, and PDF back to documents.
  • Reports in code: the fluent report API, with rows, tables, grids, styles, components and paging, and the live preview tool.
