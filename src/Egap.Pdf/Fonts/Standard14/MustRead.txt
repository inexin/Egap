Adobe Core14 font metrics
=========================

The 14 AFM files in this folder are Adobe's font metrics for the 14 PDF standard fonts, unchanged, as published at
<http://partners.adobe.com/public/developer/font/index.html> (copied from Matplotlib's pdfcorefonts folder, which keeps
them as Adobe published them). Egap.Pdf embeds them as resources to measure text in the standard fonts. This title and
this paragraph were added by the Egap authors; Adobe's notice follows unmodified.
